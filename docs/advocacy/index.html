What I work on

I lead the AI for Indigenous Communities Vertical at the Global Youth AI Advisory Body (GYAAB), where I authored a special report examining AI-driven risks and opportunities for indigenous communities globally, and contributed the chapter "Indigenous Digital Self-Determination" to AI Multipolarity: People. Peace. Planet.

The benefits of AI must be built to include everyone, not just those already positioned to capture them.

That conviction is what takes me to rooms like the AI for Good Global Summit, the ITU Academy, and UN forums on AI governance: not as a spectator, but to make sure questions of digital inclusion and indigenous self-determination are part of how these frameworks get built, not an afterthought once they are finished.
